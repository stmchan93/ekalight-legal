Ekalight

Scan, price and track your trading cards.

Point your camera at a card — raw or graded — and Ekalight identifies it and shows its current market value in seconds. Build your collection, watch its value move over time, and keep track of what you own and what you're hunting for.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Scan anything. Raw cards and graded slabs recognized by the camera, in English and Japanese.
  • Price it properly. Live market data, per condition and per grade, with price history for every card.
  • Track your collection. Add cards with one tap, see your total value, and follow gains and losses over time.
  • Know the market. Tap through to live marketplace listings and recent sold comps.
  • Share the hobby. Post your pulls, follow other collectors, and show off your best cards on a public profile.
